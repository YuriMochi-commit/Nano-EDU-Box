## 🚀 1. Executive Summary
NanoEduBox is an educational web application designed for middle school students in Myanmar to make learning interactive and engaging[cite: 13]. It helps students explore core subjects like **History, Geography, and Science**, practice **typing skills** (both English and Myanmar Pyidaungsu), and learn Myanmar's regions and states through interactive map quizzes.

---

## 🎯 2. Motivation, Vision & Mission
* **Motivation:** To provide an interactive educational platform supporting Myanmar's evolving education system, helping middle school students learn core subjects and practice digital skills at their own pace.
* **Vision:** 
  * Provide interactive tools for typing practice and digital skill enhancement.
  * Enable map-based learning for Myanmar's regions and states.
  * Foster critical thinking, problem-solving, and a lifelong interest in learning.

---

## 🌟 3. Target Users & Key Advantages
* **Students:** Interactive quizzes for History, Geography, and Science, typing games, and map exploration.
* **Parents:** Track children's learning progress and support them effectively.
* **Teachers/Schools:** Monitor student performance and provide timely feedback.
* **System Advantages:**
  * **Game-based Learning:** Typing games, Map click games, and Subject quizzes.
  * **Bilingual Support:** Supports both Burmese and English.
  * **Structured Levels:** Ranging from Beginner to Professional / KG to Grade 12.

---

## 💻 4. Project Implementation & Features
Developed using **C#.NET** with **Visual Studio** editor.

* **Admin Panel:** Secure login for administrators to manage student records, questions, and system content.
* **Brain Quiz:** Grade-based quizzes (Grade 4 to Grade 8) covering History, Geography, Science, and General Knowledge with time-bound questions and progress tracking.
* **Typo Master:** Typing practice modules for English and Myanmar Pyidaungsu across multiple difficulty levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional, Expert/Master) featuring virtual keyboards and WPM/accuracy metrics.
* **State Clicker:** Interactive map game to test and improve knowledge of Myanmar's states and regions.
